ABOUT US

Creating a community to learn, contribute, and grow.

Afghanistan Changemakers was established in July 2025 to create a community where Afghan young people can learn, contribute, and grow into leaders. Our work brings together learners, volunteer instructors, coordinators, and emerging changemakers from different provinces and backgrounds.

We go beyond providing educational access. We encourage participants to take responsibility, share knowledge, develop ideas, and create opportunities for others. A learner can become a volunteer; a volunteer can become an instructor or coordinator.

Our Mission

To expand access to education, practical skills, and leadership opportunities for Afghan young people, enabling them to participate meaningfully in their communities.

Our Vision

An Afghanistan where young people have the knowledge, confidence, and opportunities to shape their futures and contribute to an empowered society.

CORE VALUE

Education as a force for change.

Empowering Afghan youth, especially girls, to become leaders, peacebuilders, and changemakers who shape their own futures.

WHY AFGHANISTAN CHANGEMAKERS?

A different narrative. A generation of contributors.

Afghanistan is often represented through its challenges. We contribute another perspective recognizing the creativity and contributions of Afghan youth.

Beyond Challenges

We aim to create spaces where Afghan youth can demonstrate their potential through learning, leadership, and community participation.

Active Role

We are building more than educational access. We are encouraging a generation to take an active role in shaping what comes next.

Creating Opportunities

Our name reflects the belief that circumstances should not determine what young people are capable of achieving.

OUR PROGRAMMES

Learning that creates opportunities

All AC courses are offered free of charge, responding to the development needs of our community.

01

Language Education

English courses from A1 to C1, alongside Spanish and Chinese language learning to strengthen communication skills.

02

Digital Skills

Practical learning in digital skills, including website development (HTML/CSS), introducing tools for the digital world.

03

Changemakers Scholars (CSI)

CV writing, essay writing, interview preparation, and finding scholarship opportunities with expert mentors.

04

Mentorship & Guidance

Educational guidance supporting students exploring international pathways and accredited online secondary diplomas.

05

Community Learning

Workshops, discussions, and awareness activities around crucial community subjects led by members.

06 · Coming Soon

Data Science & Python

Coming soon this winter — introductory Python programming and data science fundamentals to build analytical and coding skills.

OUR JOURNEY

Building on experience. Creating new opportunities.

2022

Earlier Youth Engagement

Rising Girls Initiative and the Let Girls Go & Get Knowledge programme supported 20 Afghan girls during school closures.

2024

Alternative Learning

Creative courses, workshops, and community activities provided alternative ways to sustain learning when in-person meetings were restricted.

July 2025

Afghanistan Changemakers Established

Independently established as a broader youth-led nonprofit organization focused on free education, volunteerism, and youth leadership.

2025–2026

Growing Opportunities

Expanded course offerings, developed scholarship guidance, and created international online secondary diploma pathways for students.
